Sketch the Cloud Publisher

Privacy Policy

Sketch the Cloud Publisher · effective 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Sketch the Cloud Publisher (“the app”) accesses, uses, stores, protects, shares and deletes Google user data. The app is the publishing tool of the YouTube channel Sketch the Cloud: it uploads the channel's videos and reads their statistics through YouTube API Services. It is used only by the owner of that channel, for that one channel, and is not offered to the public.

1. Google user data the app accesses

Only after the channel owner signs in with Google and allows it, and only for the owner's own channel:

  • YouTube channel information: the channel's ID, name, handle and public statistics (number of subscribers, views and videos).
  • The channel's own videos: their IDs, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles, privacy settings and publishing times, which the app creates or updates when it uploads a video.
  • YouTube Analytics reports of the channel's own videos, such as views, watch time and average view duration.

Permissions requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl (upload and manage the channel's videos) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly (read the channel's analytics). The app never sees a Google password. It does not access email, contacts, files, other people's channels or the personal data of viewers.

2. How the app uses the data

  • To upload the channel's videos and set their details, thumbnails, subtitles and publishing time.
  • To show the channel's owner how the channel and its videos perform.

Google user data is used only for these features, which the channel owner uses directly. It is not used for advertising of any kind (targeted, personalized, retargeted or interest-based), not sold, not given to data brokers or information resellers, not used to decide credit-worthiness or for lending, and not used to build databases for any other purpose. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Sharing

Google user data is not shared, sold, transferred or disclosed to any third party. The app sends data only to Google / YouTube, when it calls the YouTube APIs on the channel owner's behalf.

Sketch the Cloud Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and protection

  • The permission the owner grants (an OAuth refresh token) and the app's credentials are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private environment. They are never written into code, logs or this website.
  • Every call to Google is made over encrypted HTTPS connections, with short-lived (one hour) access tokens.
  • Upload records (a video's YouTube ID, link and publishing time) and channel statistics are kept in the owner's private, access-controlled storage, which only the owner can reach.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Channel statistics are kept only as long as they are useful to follow the channel's growth, and at most 12 months; records of uploads are kept while the video is on YouTube.
  • When access is withdrawn (below), the app stops working at once, the stored token becomes useless and is deleted, and the stored statistics are deleted within 30 days.
  • To ask for any data to be deleted sooner, use the contact below; the request is handled within 30 days.

6. Withdrawing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Sketch the Cloud Publisher → Delete all connections).

7. Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. This website

This website collects no personal data: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no forms. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

9. Changes

If the way the app uses Google user data changes, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/realsanket/realsanket.github.io/issues or write to the channel via its About page.
