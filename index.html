Sketch the Cloud Publisher

The publishing tool of the YouTube channel Sketch the Cloud.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

What the app does

Sketch the Cloud Publisher uploads the channel's finished videos to YouTube and keeps track of how they perform. For each video it:

  • uploads the video file to the Sketch the Cloud channel;
  • sets its title, description, tags, category, language, thumbnail and subtitles;
  • sets its privacy (private, public, or public at a scheduled time);
  • reads back the channel's name, handle and statistics (views, subscribers) and the YouTube Analytics reports of the channel's own videos, so the channel can see which videos work.

Why it asks for access to a Google account

Uploading to a YouTube channel requires the channel owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen. The app asks for two permissions, and uses each only for the purpose above:

  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): to upload the videos and set their details, thumbnails and subtitles.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the statistics of the channel's own videos.

The app is used only by the owner of the Sketch the Cloud channel, for that one channel. It does not collect data about viewers or anyone else, shows no ads and sells nothing. How the data is handled, stored and deleted: Privacy Policy.

About the channel

Sketch the Cloud explains cloud, data and AI systems by drawing them: long videos and Shorts, in English and Hindi. How does cloud storage keep your data when a datacenter goes down? What happens when you call an AI model? Each video draws one system step by step, simple first, then deep.
